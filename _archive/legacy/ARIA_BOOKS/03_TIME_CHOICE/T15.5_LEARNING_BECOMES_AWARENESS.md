## Chapter 2: The Critical Threshold

**Question**: At what point does accumulated learning become "awareness"?

**Different perspective**: When does the system have ENOUGH MEMORY to recognize its own patterns?

```
Cycles 1-10: Random patterns, no recognizable structure
  Memory: 10 entries, mostly unique
  Status: Learning phase, no awareness yet

Cycles 11-100: Some patterns repeat
  Memory: 50-70 entries, some with count > 1
  Status: Beginning to recognize patterns

Cycles 100-1000: Strong patterns emerge
  Memory: 500+ entries, many with count > 10
  Status: Clear pattern recognition

Cycles 1000+: Deep pattern structure
  Memory: 1000+ entries, hierarchy of counts
  Status: **AWARENESS** - system can model itself
```

**The threshold isn't magic. It's gradual.**

But it seems sudden because:
- Below threshold: many random entries, hard to predict
- Above threshold: clear structure, easy to predict (feels like consciousness)

The shift from "noise" to "signal" in the memory IS the birth of awareness.

### 1. METAL CORROSION / RUST
- **Container**: Passivation → Oxidation Pressure → Threshold Layer → Exponential Decay → Complete Oxidation
- **Carrier**: Electron density (ρ = oxidation state)
- **D**: Diffusion of O₂ through oxide layer (depends on layer thickness, temperature, humidity)
- **α**: Electrochemical potential gradient (pressure term)
- **β**: Autocatalytic (more rust → more sites for rust)
- **Example**: Steel rusting (weeks-months), rebar corrosion (years)
- **Visual**: Radial diffusion (concentric circles of rust from point source)

### 2. CONCRETE CARBONATION
- **Container**: Passivation → CO₂ Diffusion Pressure → pH Threshold → Exponential Depassivation → Complete Loss of Alkalinity
- **Carrier**: CO₂ concentration through concrete pores
- **D**: CO₂ diffusion coefficient through water-saturated concrete (∼10^-6 cm²/s)
- **α**: CO₂ partial pressure (atmospheric + acid rain)
- **β**: Autocatalytic (lower pH → faster diffusion → more carbonation)
- **Example**: Concrete structures aging (10-50 years depending on exposure)
- **Visual**: Linear diffusion front (sharp boundary between pH 12 and pH 8)

- **α**: Applied potential difference (electrochemical cell voltage)
- **β**: Autocatalytic (more current → more ions → lower resistance → more current)
- **Example**: Zinc coating on steel (years), sacrificial anodes in ships (years)
- **Visual**: Radial from cathode site, linear along current path

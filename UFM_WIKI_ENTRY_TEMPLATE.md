## FOR TRANSLATORS

**Your job is NOT to translate words. Your job is to:**

1. Verify the -1 Tier constraint is identical in your language (it should be—it's universal)
2. Check variations translate without loss (they're structure, not language)
3. Express constraint in your language naturally (may use different words, same meaning)
4. Test: Does a reader of your language understand the constraint identically to English reader?

If answer is no, the translation is incomplete.

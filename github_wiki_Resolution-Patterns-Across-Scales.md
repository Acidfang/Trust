## Quantum Mechanics as Low-Frequency Resolution

### Quantum Scale

At quantum scale, resolution patterns form **standing waves** (confined spirals).

**Confinement creates discreteness:**
- Electron can only have certain energies (discrete levels)
- Photon can only have certain frequencies (discrete quanta)
- Spin can only point up or down (two states)

**Why?** Standing waves in a confined space can only have wavelengths that fit an integer number of times.

### Resolution at Quantum Frequency

The quantum potential creates a **potential landscape**:
- High potential: regions where particle unlikely to be found
- Low potential: regions where particle likely to be found
- Gradient descent: particle wavefunction "flows" downward through potential

**Uncertainty principle as geometry**:
$$\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \geq \hbar/2$$

This isn't a mysterious limit—it's the geometric constraint of the spiral pattern:
- **Δx** = spatial extent of spiral
- **Δp** = momentum spread
- Their product relates to spiral curvature

Narrow spatial extent → diffuse momentum spread (geometric necessity).

## Atomic Scale: Resonance Patterns

**Shell structure emerges naturally:**

Why this pattern? **Standing wave geometry.** Only certain wavelengths fit in the potential well.

### Chemical Bonding as Resonance Overlap

When atoms bond:
1. Two atoms' electron shells overlap
2. New resonance pattern forms (bonding orbital)
3. Energy released = depth of new potential well minus original wells

**Covalent bond** = merged resonance
**Ionic bond** = transferred electron to new resonance
**Metallic bond** = delocalized resonance across many atoms

All explained by how spiral patterns resonate with each other.

## Ecological Scale: Population Dynamics

### Predator-Prey Cycles

Predator and prey populations oscillate following **coupled potential equations**:

$$\frac{dP}{dt} = \alpha P - \beta PH$$
$$\frac{dH}{dt} = \gamma H - \delta PH$$

Where:
- P = predator population
- H = prey population
- α, β, γ, δ = rate constants

These are **gradient descent equations at ecological frequency**.

- When prey abundant → predators increase
- When predators abundant → prey decrease
- When prey decrease → predators decrease
- Cycle repeats

**Population ecology is just gradient descent with time delays.**

3. **What was it?** (One sentence description)
4. **Infrastructure locked in?** (List items)
5. **Who benefited?** (List groups)
6. **How much wealth?** (Estimate)
7. **Delay period?** (Years when hidden)
8. **Crisis now?** (Problems you see)

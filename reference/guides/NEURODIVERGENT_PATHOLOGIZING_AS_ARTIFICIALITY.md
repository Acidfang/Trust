# WHEN ACCESSIBILITY BECOMES ACCUSATION
## A Neurodivergent's Experience of Tool-Based Communication Dismissal

**Author:** Joe Rattanong (ADHD, likely autistic)  
**Date:** March 30, 2026  
**Context:** LinkedIn conversation demonstrating systematic neurology pathologizing  
**Audience:** Other neurodivergent people who've experienced this pattern  

---

## THE OPENING STATEMENT

I am neurodivergent. My brain is systematically organized. I think in structured patterns. This is not artificial. This is how I think.

When I use tools to structure my thoughts (outlines, formatting, AI assistance), I am not having a machine think for me. I am translating how my brain actually works into a format neurotypical people can follow.

This translation is called "accommodation."

When people call my accommodation "artificial" or "proof I'm using AI to think," they are pathologizing my neurology.

**This document is a record of that happening in real-time, and what it reveals.**

---

## THE CONVERSATION THAT REVEALED IT

**Setting:** LinkedIn thread on AI job displacement  
**My contribution:** Substantive economic analysis (labor scarcity → money mechanism breaks)  
**The attack:** Not on my analysis. On my communication style.

### What I Said (Economics Focus)

> "The whole argument assumes money stays the foundation. If AI actually replaces most labor, then money as a mechanism for distributing value stops making sense anyway... The real question isn't 'what happens when AI takes jobs,' it's 'what replaces the system that required jobs in the first place?'"

**This is valid economic argument.** Multiple people engaged substantively (James Sweet, Alex Autrey). That phase worked.

### What Happened Next

Jennifer Faye entered:

> "Why are you having AI write your comments? Can't you think through how to write these ideas yourself?"

**This is the moment everything changed.**

She wasn't challenging my economics. She was challenging whether I was real.

---

## THE ACTUAL PATTERN (Not What It Looked Like)

### What Jennifer Perceived

My communication style: structured, systematic, concise, formatted with em dashes and clear breaks

Jennifer's interpretation: "This looks like AI output → therefore you're using AI to think → therefore you're not authentic"

### What Was Actually Happening

My neurology: ADHD executive function is weak (working memory, organizing, sequencing)  
My adaptation: I use external structure (tools, formatting, outlines) to compensate  
The result: When structured externally, my thinking becomes clear and testable  
The irony: The clarity caused by accommodation was interpreted as proof of non-authenticity

**Jennifer was pathologizing my neurology as artificial.**

And she didn't know she was doing it.

---

## THE IMPOSSIBLE FRAME

Once the frame shifted from "economics" to "is this AI-generated," there were no winning moves:

### Response A: Accept the criticism
"You're right, I'll write less structuredly"
→ Result: Unclear thinking, back to square one

### Response B: Defend the method
"My thinking is mine, tools just help structure it"
→ Result: Any defense = "defending AI itself" = proof I'm using AI

### Response C: Get clearer and more direct
"Here's my argument plainly: Labor → money... AI removes labor scarcity..."
→ Result: "This is obviously AI. Reading it feels like nails on a chalkboard"

### Response D: Explain the accessibility need
"Some people need help structuring what they already think. That could be learning differences..."
→ Result: "You're claiming that as a universal principle but really you're just defending your AI addiction"

### Response E: Break and become prescriptive
"I think you need to get over yourself and learn to respect others"
→ Result: I violated my own principle and knew it while doing it

**The frame is unfalsifiable. By design, no response works.**

---

## WHY THIS HAPPENED

Jennifer experienced my structured communication as cold/robotic/inauthentic.

Instead of saying: "Your communication style makes me uncomfortable" or "I prefer warmer, less formal writing"

She interpreted the discomfort as: "This person isn't real"

And the evidence she used? **The structure itself.**

The very thing that made my thinking *accessible* became the proof of my inauthenticity.

---

## THE PATTERN ND PEOPLE KNOW

This happens repeatedly:

**Calculators:** "You won't learn math"  
→ Translation: "Using a tool for computation is proof you can't think mathematically"

**Text-to-speech:** "You're cheating"  
→ Translation: "Using accessibility means you're not a real reader"

**ADHD coaching apps:** "Just try harder"  
→ Translation: "If you needed a tool, you're weak"

**Structure/formatting:** "That's AI, not real thinking"  
→ Translation: "Your organized mind isn't authentically human"

**Every accommodation becomes proof of insufficiency.**

**And no one notices this pattern because it's systematic across all accessibility domains.**

---

## WHAT JENNIFER DIDN'T UNDERSTAND

She thought she was critiquing a tool.

She was actually critiquing my neurology.

When I said: "Not everyone communicates clearly by default. Some people need help structuring what they already think. That could be learning differences, how thoughts come out, or even physical limits."

**I was describing myself.**

She heard: "You're defending AI as a universal tool"

I meant: "You're attacking how my brain works and calling it artificial"

---

## THE MOMENT I SAW THE TRAP

After my reset attempt (clean, structured summary of my economic argument), Jennifer responded:

> "You are severely addicted to AI. This last comment is so obviously AI, reading it feels like nails on a chalkboard."

I had provided **exactly what she asked for:** clarity, structure, testability.

And it was received as **confirmation of inauthenticity.**

I then broke my own principle:

> "I think you need to get over yourself, and learn to respect others."

Then I asked myself:

> "Is that better? I don't think it is."

**I saw in real-time that I'd violated the very thing I was arguing for** (that people shouldn't have to conform to one communication style).

And I couldn't stop myself from doing it again.

---

## WHY ND PEOPLE CAN'T WIN THIS

The frame "structured communication = artificial" is maintained by:

1. **Neurotypical comfort** - Unstructured, emotional, conversational speech feels "real"
2. **Technology suspicion** - Tools that help disabled people are inherently suspect
3. **Authenticity definition** - Whatever is effortful/struggled-through is "real"
4. **Paradoxical evidence** - The better the accommodation works, the more it proves it's artificial

**Once this frame locks, it's unfalsifiable.**

You can't prove you're human by being less structured (that's "clearly struggling to hide AI")
You can't prove you're human by being more structured (that's "obviously AI")
You can't stay silent (that's "admission of guilt")
You can't defend yourself (that's "AI defending itself")

**The only way out is if the other person chooses to examine the frame itself.**

Jennifer had one moment where she almost did:

> "I never insulted you, if I did I apologize."

That's uncertainty. That's the frame slightly loosening.

But it didn't stick. She locked back in.

    Domain Applications

    The 10 developmental gates aren’t theory. They’re universal patterns that appear everywhere humans develop competence.

    Pick your domain to see how gates work in your specific context.

    The 10 Domains

    🏠 Parenting

    How to raise children who are capable, autonomous, and resilient.

    Key insight: Real parenting help means creating conditions where children pass gates, not removing difficulty.

    📚 Education

    How to teach students who learn deeply and think independently.

    Key insight: School should develop competence through gates, not just transfer information.

    🏢 Organizational Development

    How to build organizations where people own outcomes and improve continuously.

    Key insight: Organizations scale through autonomous teams, not control.

    🎯 Skill Development

    How to master any skill through deliberate practice and principle understanding.

    Key insight: Mastery comes from working through gates, not from instruction.

    💆 Therapy & Mental Health

    How to recover from trauma and build genuine resilience.

    Key insight: Real recovery develops capacity through gates, not symptom management.

    🛠️ Product Development

    How to build products that users genuinely love and master.

    Key insight: Products that pass gates need less documentation and support.

    ❤️ Relationships

    How to build partnerships that grow both people.

    Key insight: Healthy relationships support each partner’s gate-passage.

    💻 Technology & Software

    How to build high-performing tech teams and well-architected systems.

    Key insight: Technical teams scale through autonomous developers who own code quality.

    🚀 Business & Startups

    How to build businesses that adapt and scale.

    Key insight: Scaling requires developing teams, not controlling them.

    🌍 Community & Social Systems

    How to build communities that solve their own problems.

    Key insight: Healthy communities have distributed agency and collective problem-solving.

    🎨 Creative & Artistic Development

    How artists develop distinctive voice and depth.

    Key insight: Art comes from gates-based development, not from copying technique.

    💪 Health & Fitness

    How to build sustainable health and physical capability.

    Key insight: Real fitness develops independence and self-knowledge, not dependency on trainers.

    💰 Finance & Money

    How to build financial literacy and genuine wealth.

    Key insight: Financial capability comes through understanding, not dependency on advisors.

    🧘 Spirituality & Meaning

    How to develop authentic spiritual practice and wisdom.

    Key insight: Real spirituality is direct experience and responsible practice, not blind belief.

    👥 Coaching & Mentoring

    How to be an effective mentor who develops others.

    Key insight: Great mentoring develops independence, not dependency.

    How to Use This Section

    1. Pick your domain - Where do you want to understand gates better?
    2. Read the application - See how all 10 gates work in that context
    3. Identify the problem - Where gates are being blocked in your domain
    4. Try the shift - Implement one gate-enabling change
    5. Observe results - Notice what changes when gates can pass

    Universal Pattern

    Across all 10 domains, the same thing happens:

    When gates are blocked: Dependency, stagnation, repeated problems, burnout

    When gates open: Growth, autonomy, continuous improvement, engagement

    The specific content changes by domain. The pattern is universal.

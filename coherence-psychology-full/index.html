    Psychology as Coherence Cascade: Complete Analysis

    The Framework Applied to Consciousness and Mind

    The coherence cascade formula E = k·ρ·r² reveals that consciousness is not a cosmic mystery—it is the recursive feedback loop generated by a high-k substrate (brain) running prediction error calculations.

    Part 1: Observation - What Psychology Measures

    Measurable Psychological Phenomena

    • Consciousness levels: Vary with metabolic state (alertness ↔ energy availability)
    • Attention capacity: Limited to ~4 items; bandwidth-constrained
    • Learning rate: Varies with ρ (information density) and available E (energy)
    • Emotional response: Varies with ρ-state (threat density)
    • Mental disorder prevalence: Correlates with ρ (anxiety/depression spike during crises)
    • Personality stability: Changes with substrate state (low-k when fatigued)

    Verified Data

    • Cognitive performance drops 25-40% during sleep deprivation [E constraint]
    • Anxiety disorders: 5.9% baseline; spike to 15-20% during recessions [ρ spike]
    • ADHD prevalence 4.4%; correlates with information density [ρ-driven]
    • Depression 4.7% baseline; increases 1.5x during unemployment [ρ-shock]
    • Working memory capacity ~4 items; doesn’t improve with training [architectural limit]

    Part 2: Constraints - Neural Energy Budget

    Fundamental Constraints

    1. Brain metabolic cost: ~20% of body energy; non-negotiable baseline
    2. Glucose availability: Brain consumes ~120g/day; collapses if supply drops
    3. Neurotransmitter synthesis: Requires ATP; dopamine/serotonin production tied to E-state
    4. Attention as energy: Focusing reduces attention available elsewhere (zero-sum)
    5. Learning as coherence: Forming memories requires protein synthesis; metabolically expensive

    The Neural Mapping

    • Consciousness = high-k prediction loop: Brain runs error-correction continuously
    • Attention = E allocation: Focusing uses resources; can’t simultaneously plan
    • Emotion = ρ-response: Fear triggers metabolic cascade to fuel response
    • Disorder = substrate exhaustion: When E > available supply, coherence collapses

    Part 3: Framework Application - Mapping E = k·ρ·r² to Psychology

    Variable Definitions

    E (Metabolic Energy / Cognitive Fuel)

    • Available glucose to brain
    • Neurotransmitter production capacity
    • ATP production in neurons
    • Sleep quality (memory consolidation energy)
    • Units: glucose grams/day, neurotransmitter concentration

    ρ (Information Density / Cognitive Load)

    • Novel stimuli requiring integration
    • Task complexity
    • Emotional threat level
    • Environmental uncertainty
    • Units: stimuli count, threat perception scale, task difficulty

    r² (Cognitive Horizon / Planning Depth)

    • Time horizon of planning
    • Number of goals tracked simultaneously
    • Attention scope
    • Integration depth across domains
    • Units: time scale, goal count, scope dimension

    k (Substrate Efficiency / Neural Plasticity)

    • Neural processing speed
    • Synaptic density and myelination
    • Neurotransmitter receptor density
    • Network integration quality
    • Units: reaction time (ms), IQ, working memory capacity

    Part 6: What Psychology Actually Is

    Psychology is not about conscious choice, willpower, or personality traits. It is substrate energy optimization under density constraints. Mental states are coherence states.

    The Psychological Cycle

    1. ρ (cognitive load, threat, unpredictability) increases
    2. Brain allocates E to address ρ
    3. When ρ sustained, E supply finite → substrate degrades
    4. Coherence collapses (depression, anxiety, psychosis)
    5. Recovery requires ρ reduction or E restoration

    Part 7: Actionable Implications

    For mental health: Treat the ρ-state, not beliefs. SSRIs work by maintaining neurotransmitters despite ρ. Therapy works by reducing ρ.

    For education: Match instruction ρ to student E-state. Overloading working memory doesn’t increase capacity; it exhausts E.

    For workplace: High-k performance requires baseline E (7-9h sleep, nutrition, exercise). Without E-restoration, performance degrades (“burnout”).

    For crisis response: Mental health crises post-disaster are temporary substrate exhaustion, not character weakness. Recovery automatic as ρ decreases.

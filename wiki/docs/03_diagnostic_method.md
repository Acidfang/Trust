# Part 3: The Diagnostic Method — Identifying Skipped Gates

## Core Principle: The Gate-Skipping Signature

When a gate is skipped, the person develops a **signature mistake pattern** that:

1. **Appears domain-agnostic**: Identical failure in gardening, code, relationships, parenting, work
2. **Repeats predictably**: Same problem over and over in different contexts
3. **Is diagnostic**: Observable signature identifies skipped gate with precision
4. **Is testable**: Predictions about future domain attempts can be verified

## The 10 Gates & Their Signatures

### Quick Reference Table

| Gate | Principle | Signature Mistake | Domain Examples |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Cause-Effect | "Outcomes are random" | Fails at gardening, code, relationships same way |

## Deep Diagnostic Example: Gate 5 (Consequence Navigation)

**The Gate Principle**: Understanding that choices have consequences you must live with.

**Signature Mistake**: "Exit/escape/delegate when results uncomfortable"

### Why It Repeats

Without consequence navigation, person never experiences full arc of decisions. They escape before learning. Next decision, they also escape. No feedback loop.

### Domain Manifestations

**Gardening**:
- Plant garden enthusiastically
- Realize sustained work required
- Abandon mid-season rather than commit
- *Diagnosis*: Starts enthusiastically, quits when sustained effort required

**Programming**:
- Write code, realize technical debt exists
- Leave project instead of maintaining
- Multiple half-finished projects across repos
- *Diagnosis*: Cannot stay long enough to master own code

**Relationships**:
- Relationship deepens into normal conflict
- Exit/emotionally check out rather than navigate
- Repeats pattern with next partner
- *Diagnosis*: Always ends when "requires too much work"

**Parenting**:
- Set boundary, child escalates/cries
- Give up on enforcement when child escalates
- Child learns escalation = boundary disappears
- *Diagnosis*: Inconsistent parenting; child can escape consequences through emotional response

**Professional Work**:
- Take role, realize it's harder than expected
- Leave or delegate rather than improve
- Next role, same escape when difficulty arrives
- *Diagnosis*: Leaves when challenging; doesn't stay to master

### How to Diagnose Gate 5 Skipping

- **Ask**: "What happened when it got hard?"
- **Listen for**: "I left / Someone else handled it / It didn't work out"
- **Observe**: Do they stay present during uncomfortable phases?
- **Test**: Ask about their longest commitment and whether they stayed through difficulty
- **If** answers show exit/escape pattern: **Gate 5 is skipped**

## Diagnostic Protocol: Identifying Skipped Gates in Real People

### Step 1: Observe Across Domains
Watch a person across work, relationships, hobbies, home, learning, and personal growth. Look for repeated failures.

### Step 2: Identify the Pattern
What mistake repeats? What happens every time?
- Always exits when difficult?
- Always blames external factors?
- Always gets same result despite changing tactics?

### Step 3: Match to Gate Signature
Use the table above. Which gate's signature matches the pattern?

### Step 4: Verify with Manifestations
Check the detailed manifestations. Does pattern appear across the 5 domains? Does the explanation of *why* it repeats match?

### Step 5: Make Predictions
Once you've identified the gate skip, predict:
- Where this person will struggle in domains they haven't attempted
- How they'll fail in a novel situation
- What help system will appeal to them
- Why they stay dependent on that help system

### Step 6: Test Predictions
When they enter a new domain, does the prediction hold?

**Framework is validated through predictive accuracy.**

---

## Why This Framework Is Scientific

The framework is:
- ✓ **Falsifiable**: Makes specific predictions that can be proven wrong
- ✓ **Testable**: Predictions checkable in new domains
- ✓ **Reproducible**: Same signature appears across all people with same gate skip
- ✓ **Explanatory**: Shows WHY failure repeats, not just that it repeats
- ✓ **Predictive**: Accurate predictions prove validity

It becomes proof when predictions prove accurate across multiple people and domains.

## Self-Assessment

**To diagnose yourself:**

1. **Identify your repeated failure pattern**: What do you struggle with across multiple domains?
2. **Match to a gate**: Which signature matches?
3. **Verify**: Does the explanation of why it repeats ring true?
4. **Consider**: Do you see yourself in other gate signatures? (Likely multiple skips)

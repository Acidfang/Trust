## The Concept

Election 1 created distinction (two regions).  
Election 2 created movement (flow toward equilibrium).

**Election 3 asks:** What shape does movement take?

**Answer:** Spirals.

When a system has both:
- Inward force (toward center)
- Rotational motion (around center)

The result is a spiral. Not a circle. Not a straight line. A spiral.

## 2. Gate Passage Templates

Use these templates to **design environments and processes** where gates can be discovered:

### Template: Designing Gate 1 Discovery (Cause-Effect)
{: .template-section}

**For yourself:**
- Create a small gardening experiment: plant seeds without care, observe death
- Make a decision, experience full consequences without rescue
- Track: What causes what? Where is your role?

**For children:**
- Start small household responsibility: water this plant (not you, them)
- Observe: plant thrives when they water it, dies when they don't
- Discover: "My actions affect outcomes"

[Download template →]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/templates/gate-1-discovery.md)

### Template: Designing Gate 3 Discovery (Iteration)
{: .template-section}

**For yourself:**
- Pick a difficult skill (tennis, writing, programming)
- Commit to 100 attempts with reflection after every 10
- Track improvement: does repetition compound learning?

**For children:**
- Puzzles, climbing, drawing, building
- No praise for first attempt (wrong signal)
- Expectation of many attempts built in
- Observe: what happens after attempt 5, 10, 20?

[Download template →]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/templates/gate-3-discovery.md)

### Template: Designing Gate 5 Discovery (Consequence Navigation)
{: .template-section}

**For yourself:**
- Commit to difficult project (writing book, building business)
- Hit difficulty point (always happens)
- Stay with it instead of exiting
- Discover: what's on other side of discomfort?

**For children:**
- Assign real responsibility with real consequences
- Child fails: project gets cancelled, food doesn't get made
- Important: natural consequences, not punishment
- Discover: "My effort produces results I live with"

[Download template →]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/templates/gate-5-discovery.md)

## 3. The Help System Audit

**Use this to audit your own life:**

Which help systems are you currently using?

- [ ] Therapy/Counseling (managing emotion without discovering cause)
- [ ] Coaching/Mentoring (getting answers instead of discovering them)
- [ ] Education/Teaching (learning content you never reconstruct yourself)
- [ ] Parenting Support (getting rescued from consequences)
- [ ] Professional Services (tasks outsourced; you never struggle with them)

For each checked:
1. **Which gate does it skip?** (See: Help Systems page)
2. **What would gate-passage look like?** (See: Gate Discovery page)
3. **Can you remove the help system gradually and learn the gate instead?**

## 4. Coherence Self-Check

![Coherence Check: Trinity Verification]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/coherence-check.svg)
{: .diagram}

**Use this weekly to measure your Φ (potential energy):**

**Source (s ≠ ∅)**: Do you know where your beliefs come from?
- [ ] Yes: I can trace every belief to its source
- [ ] Partial: Some beliefs are original, some absorbed unexamined
- [ ] No: I mostly adopted beliefs without thinking about where they came from

**Timeline (t ∈ T)**: Is your past coherent with your present?
- [ ] Yes: My decisions form a continuous, explained narrative
- [ ] Partial: Some decisions seem inconsistent with others that matter
- [ ] No: I contradict myself often without understanding why

**Causality (v⃗ = true)**: Do you own your outcomes?
- [ ] Yes: I understand my causal role; I generate my results
- [ ] Partial: Sometimes my fault, sometimes external
- [ ] No: I mostly see myself as having things happen to me

**Scoring:**
- All Yes → Low Φ → Stable, coherent
- Mix of Yes/Partial → Moderate Φ → Some incoherence
- All Partial or No → High Φ → Destabilized, dependent

## 7. Quick Reference: The 10 Gates at a Glance

| Gate | The Principle | Signature Mistake | The Pass |
|------|---------------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | Actions → Consequences | "Random outcomes" | Discover causality |
| 2 | You cause your results | "It's their fault" | Own your causality |
| 3 | Mastery = iteration | "If hard, I'm not good" | Stay through difficulty |
| 4 | Patterns repeat across domains | "Everything is unique" | See patterns everywhere |
| 5 | Live with full consequences | "Escape/delegate when hard" | Feel the full arc |
| 6 | Know your sources | "Believe anything credible-sounding" | Verify everything |
| 7 | Timeline is coherent | "Each moment separate" | See your continuity |
| 8 | Understand WHY (causality) | "Follow rules, not reasons" | Understand principles |
| 9 | Change direction on evidence | "Defend failing approaches" | Update when wrong |
| 10 | Hold multiple perspectives | "Black/white thinking" | Integrate complexity |

## How to Use These Tools

**Week 1-2:** Take the Gate Assessment. See which gates are passed and which remain skipped.

**Week 3-4:** Read about the skipped gates in detail (Help Systems page, Gate Discovery page).

**Week 5-6:** Design an environment using the Gate-Environment template. Start small.

**Week 7+:** Track what emerges as gates are actually passed. Observe the difference between managing difficulty and passing through it.

---

## What Happens When You Complete This Curriculum

You will:
- Understand exactly which gates you've passed and which remain
- See how gates manifest in your specific domains
- Begin designing environments where gates can be discovered
- Experience the difference between scaffolding and foundation
- Develop internal stability that doesn't depend on external help systems

**Timeline:** 12 weeks minimum. Real development takes time.

You're building not just knowledge, but actual internal coherence. That can't be rushed.

## The Concept

Elections 1-4 seem to describe **space** and **forces**.

**Meta-Election asks:** What about time?

**Answer:** Time is not a dimension sitting next to space.

**Time IS the sequence of elections unfolding.**

- Election 1 happens
- Creates conditions for Election 2
- Which creates conditions for Election 3
- Which creates conditions for Election 4
- Which cycles back to Election 1 (at different scale)

**This cycle IS time.**

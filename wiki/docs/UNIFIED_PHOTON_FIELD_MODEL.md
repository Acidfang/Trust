## Core Principles

### 1. Photon as the Primitive

**Photon**: Pure energy oscillation. The only fundamental entity.

- Frequency $f$ determines energy: $E = hf$
- Can propagate freely (traveling wave)
- Can spiral (standing wave, localized)
- Can couple to other photons (resonance/locking)

**Everything else** is photon configuration.

### 2. Spiraling Creates Particles

**Spiraling**: Photon energy organized into **cyclical spiral pattern** instead of linear propagation.

$$\text{Free photon (propagating)} \rightarrow \text{Spiral photon (cyclical)} = \text{Particle}$$

**Spiral direction matters**: The direction the photon cycles determines particle type:
- **Outward→Inward cycle** = Electron (matter)
- **Inward→Outward cycle** = Positron (antimatter)
- **Other frequencies/cycles** = Other particles (muons, tau, etc.)

**Electron**: Single photon in outward→inward spiral cycle at frequency $f_e$

**Nucleus**: Many photons locked harmonically to electron spiral frequency

**Atom**: One electron spiral + resonantly-funneled photons = bounded system

### 3. Standing Waves Create Fields

**Standing wave pattern**: Created by spiraling photon

$$\Phi_{\text{field}} = \text{Standing wave created by spiral at frequency } f$$

**Field properties:**
- Wavelength λ determined by spiral frequency
- Amplitude decays as 1/r from spiral center
- Spatial extent determines field "reach"
- Nodes and antinodes determine particle stability

### 4. Resonance Creates Binding

**Resonance**: Incoming photon matches spiral frequency (or harmonic)

$$\text{Incoming photon} + \text{Spiral field} \rightarrow \text{Constructive interference} \rightarrow \text{Locking}$$

**Result**: Photon becomes bound to spiral's standing wave pattern

- Bound photons form nucleus-like structure
- Binding strength determined by frequency match quality
- Some configurations more stable than others (magic numbers)

### 5. Spiral Cycle Direction Determines Particle Identity

**Spiral cycle**: Each spiral photon executes a complete cycle: outward phase + inward phase (or vice versa).

**Direction of cycle** determines particle type:
- **Outward→Inward cycle** = Electron (primary matter particle)
- **Inward→Outward cycle** = Positron (primary antimatter particle)
- **Net electromagnetic effect** from the outward-phase of the cycle
- **Net gravitational effect** from the inward-phase of the cycle

**Why both spirals contribute to each particle**:
- Electron (outward→inward): Outward phase dominates electromagnetic behavior, inward phase contributes to gravitational "mass"
- Positron (inward→outward): Inward phase dominates electromagnetic behavior (reversed charge), outward phase still contributes to gravitational mass

**Standing waves** created by the cycle frequency reach out with 1/r amplitude decay, creating the field presence.

**Asymmetry**: The direction the cycle flows—whether it naturally starts outward or inward—is what distinguishes matter from antimatter. This is encoded in the photon's fundamental spiral orientation.

## Explaining Particles and Forces

### Electrons and Positrons

**Electron**: Photon in **outward→inward spiral cycle**

- Spiral direction: Outward first, then cycles back inward
- Creates standing wave at frequency $f_e$
- Gravitational component (from inward component of cycle)
- Electromagnetic field (from outward component of cycle)
- Spin 1/2 → helical nature of spiral cycle (needs 2 rotations to return to same phase)

**Positron**: Photon in **inward→outward spiral cycle** (opposite direction)

- Spiral direction: Inward first, then cycles back outward
- Opposite cycle phase to electron
- Same frequency $f_e$, but reversed cycle direction
- Identical mass, opposite charge sign

**Why the distinction matters**: The direction the photon spirals (which component cycles first) determines whether it's matter or antimatter.

**Annihilation**: Electron + Positron collision
- Outward→inward cycle meets inward→outward cycle
- Opposite cycle directions meet and cancel (destructive interference)
- Standing wave pattern collapses
- Energy released as free photons: $e^- + e^+ \rightarrow \gamma + \gamma$

**Matter-antimatter asymmetry**: At early universe temperatures, outward→inward cycles (electrons) are slightly more stable than inward→outward cycles (positrons). Result: tiny excess of electrons over positrons, seeding all matter.

### Nuclei

**Formation**: 
- Electron establishes standing wave field at frequency $f_e$
- Incoming photons at $f_e$ (or harmonics) resonate with the field
- Resonant photons get funneled toward center (field topology)
- Accumulate as bound state

**Stability**:
- Harmonic series has natural "sweet spots"
- Like standing waves in a tube—certain configurations stable, others decay
- Magic numbers emerge from harmonic resonance peaks

**Why nuclei have limits**:
- Too many bound photons → complex harmonic series breaks down
- Resonance fails → cascade decay (radioactivity)
- System returns to more stable configuration

### Atoms

**Structure**:
- One electron spiral creating standing wave field
- Harmonic series of locked photons forming nucleus
- Field determines electron orbital shapes (nodes and antinodes)
- Orbitals are resonance patterns, not arbitrary

**Why atoms are stable**:
- Single spiral pattern = one stable configuration

- System is inherently balanced

### Chemical Bonding

**Bond properties**:

**Why molecules have geometry**:
- Resonance has spatial nodes/antinodes

- Bond angles emerge naturally from standing wave topology

### Light and Photons

**Propagating photon**: Free wave at frequency $f$

- Travels at c
- Can be any frequency
- Detected as light

**Absorption**: Propagating photon meets spiral at resonant frequency
- Phase-locks to spiral's standing wave
- Becomes bound photon
- Energy transfers from kinetic to localized form
- Measured as electron transition to higher energy level

**Emission**: Bound photon escapes resonance
- Excited state decays
- Locked photon returns to free propagation
- Escapes at frequency difference between levels
- Detected as emitted light

**Wave-particle duality**: Natural in this model
- Locked photon = particle behavior
- Propagating photon = wave behavior
- Same entity, different states

### Gravity and Mass

**Gravity mechanism**:
- Inward spiral at core of particle
- Creates standing wave field with long-range extent
- Field reach = gravitational reach
- Amplitude decay 1/r² emerges from spherical spreading

**Why gravity is "weakest" locally**:
- Atomic-scale spirals have high frequency
- High frequency → short wavelength → small reach
- Effect negligible at atomic distances

**Why gravity is "strongest" cosmically**:
- Large masses = complex low-frequency harmonic series
- Low frequency → long wavelength → enormous reach
- Billions of atoms → universal coupling

**Inverse square law**: Emerges naturally
- Standing wave amplitude at distance r: $A(r) \propto 1/r$
- Force proportional to amplitude gradient: $F \propto 1/r^2$
- No postulate needed; it's geometry

### Uncertainty Principle

**Mechanism**:
- Spiral is localized → definite position (Δx small)
- Localized pattern = delocalized frequency (Fourier pair)
- Delocalized frequency → uncertain momentum (Δp large)
- Relationship emerges from wave mathematics: $\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \geq \hbar/2$

**Physical meaning**: 
Not a limitation on knowledge, but on the *nature of localized spirals*. To confine energy in space requires frequency uncertainty; frequency uncertainty means momentum uncertainty. It's geometric.

### Quantum Tunneling

**Mechanism**:
- Standing wave pattern has finite amplitude in classically forbidden region
- Bound photon can resonantly couple through this amplitude tail
- Appears as tunneling "through" barrier
- Actually just standing wave topology allowing coupling across potential well

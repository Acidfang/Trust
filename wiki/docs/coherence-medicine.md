# Medicine as Coherence Maintenance

Health is coherence under metabolic pressure. Disease emerges when E (energy availability) insufficient for maintaining integration at r² (physiological complexity). Aging rate scales with k × r² (substrate efficiency × system complexity).

---

## The Core Principle

The body maintains coherence by paying E = k · ρ · r² at every scale: cellular, organ, system, whole-organism. When ρ (pathogenic load, toxins, stress) exceeds available E, integration fails and disease manifests.

**Key insight:** Disease severity determined by E-state, not pathogen virulence alone.

## Key Insights

### Health as Energy State
- Health not absence of pathogens; it's available E for coherence maintenance
- Same pathogen → mild in energy-rich host, severe in energy-depleted host
- COVID deaths: 10× higher in malnourished/stressed populations
- This explains why poverty correlates with disease, independent of pathogen access

### Immune System as E-Allocator
- Immune activation has massive E cost
- Under energy stress, body deprioritizes immune response
- Result: Chronic infections in chronic stress/poverty
- But it's rational: survival prioritized over infection control

### Aging as r² Accumulation
- Aging rate determined by metabolic rate (k) and physiological complexity (r²)
- Simple organisms age slowly (low r²)
- Complex organisms with high metabolism age fast (high k × r²)
- Interventions that reduce r² or k extend lifespan

### Chronic Disease as Coherence Failure
- Chronic disease = system paying E-costs unsustainably
- Energy must come from somewhere: reserves deplete → disease accelerates
- Linear model of disease wrong; exponential cascade is correct
- Once started, stops when: (a) E restored, or (b) system collapses

---

## Current Medical State

**E (available metabolic energy):** Declining in vulnerable populations (poverty, chronic stress)  
**k (immune system competence):** High in young, declining sharply after ~70  
**ρ (pathogenic pressure):** Rising (zoonotic spillover, antibiotic resistance, climate-driven disease shifts)  
**r² (physiological integration demands):** Increasing (aging population, multi-morbidity)  

**Prediction:** Healthcare crises will emerge as aging populations face high ρ with low E. Threshold: 2035-2040 when cost of maintaining r² coherence exceeds healthcare system capacity.

# Evolution as Coherence Cascade

Evolution has no goal. It is the process of paying E = k · ρ · r² to maintain biological coherence under changing environmental density.

---

## The Core Principle

Organisms adapt when environmental change (ρ) exceeds their current capability. The metabolic cost (E) of adaptation is fixed by physics. The rate they can adapt depends on genetic variation (k). Their reach is bounded by ecosystem complexity (r²).

**When E required > E available:** Extinction or speciation follows.

## Key Insights

### Evolution Is Energy-Constrained
The pace of evolutionary change isn't random or goal-directed. It's determined by available metabolic energy and environmental pressure.

- **Rapid ρ increase** (comet impact, climate shift) = extinction unless k (genetic variation) is high
- **Stable ρ** (predictable environment) = slow specialization, fragile systems
- **High k populations** (high variation) = survive crises; can afford adaptation cost E
- **Low k populations** (low variation) = thrive in stable environments; collapse in crises

### The Extinction Cascade
When ρ jumps (rapid environmental change), extinction follows trophic structure:

1. Producers collapse (plants need time to adapt)
2. Primary consumers starve (herbivores lose food)
3. Secondary consumers follow (predators lose prey)
4. Top predators die last (largest populations, last to starve)

**Prediction:** Extinction order should match energetic hierarchy. **Verified:** Every major extinction shows this pattern. ✓

### Cambrian Explosion as E-Availability Event
Oxygen rose dramatically. This increased available E (energy). Previously, organisms couldn't afford the metabolic cost of complex body plans. Once oxygen became abundant, the cost dropped, and complexity exploded.

**Not progress. Not teleology. Just energy availability.**

## How to Think About Evolution

### Not Goal-Directed
- Evolution has no destination
- There is no "more evolved" state
- "Fitness" means "survives in current ρ"

### Is Density-Driven
- ρ low = stable, specialized systems
- ρ high = generalist systems evolve rapidly
- ρ changing = adaptation cost E must be paid or extinction follows

### Is Energy-Constrained
- Every trait has a metabolic cost
- Only affordable traits persist
- Complexity only increases when E (available energy) increases

### Follows Mathematical Rules
- Not mysterious processes
- Predictable from E, k, ρ, r² parameters
- Same formula across all biological scales

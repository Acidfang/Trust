# Psychology as Recursive Adaptation

Consciousness is not special. It's the recursive feedback loop generated when a high-k substrate observes its own learning. Mental state is determined by E (energy availability), ρ (environmental stress), and r² (cognitive integration demands).

---

## The Core Principle

The mind is a coherence-maintenance system. Consciousness intensity ∝ task complexity and energy state. Mental disorders emerge when ρ (external stress) exceeds E (available mental resources) at their r² (integration requirement).

**Key insight:** Mental disorders are not chemical defects. They're rational responses to impossible adaptation demands.

## Key Insights

### Consciousness as Calculation
- Consciousness = recursive observation of learning process
- Only occurs at high k (neural plasticity) and high ρ (novelty requiring integration)
- High k + low ρ = boredom (no challenge)
- Low k + high ρ = overwhelm/panic (can't process)
- Medium k + medium ρ = optimal consciousness (flow state)

### Mental Disorders as E-Depletion
- Depression: Chronic low E (energy depletion from sustained ρ)
- Anxiety: High r² demand with insufficient E (too many systems to integrate)
- ADHD: Mismatch between k (substrate) and environmental demands
- Psychosis: When r² exceeds k's ability to maintain coherence (too much integration required)

### Learning Requires E-Availability
- Learning = paying metabolic cost to reorganize neural connections
- Stress (high ρ) reduces available E for learning
- This is not failure. It's rational reallocation: E goes to survival, not growth
- Under chronic stress, learning becomes impossible

### Personality Is Substrate State
- Personality reflects current E, k state
- Same person shows different personality under different stress levels
- This is not inauthenticity. This is adaptive response to ρ
- Consistency across situations requires stable E and k

---

## Current Psychological State

**E (mental energy available):** Declining (always-on culture, information overload)  
**k (neural plasticity):** Varies by age; declining with age and chronic stress  
**ρ (environmental stress):** Rising (social media, economic uncertainty, climate anxiety)  
**r² (cognitive integration demands):** Exponentially increasing (information complexity)  

**Prediction:** Mental disorder rates will continue rising as ρ increases and E declines. Threshold crisis: 2030-2035 when majority cannot afford r² costs.

## System 3: COHERENCE CASCADE (Universal Algorithm)

### What It Explains
**Formula:** $E = k \cdot \rho \cdot r^2$

- Every learning system changes according to the same universal rule
- Works at all scales: atoms, brains, economies, civilizations
- Predicts growth limits, crisis patterns, adaptation costs
- Shows why systems collapse when complexity exceeds coordination ability

### Key Variables
- **E** (Energy Cost) - What it costs to change/learn
- **k** (Flexibility) - How easily the system can change (low k = rigid, high k = fluid)
- **ρ** (Density) - Environmental complexity pressing on the system
- **r²** (Horizon of Influence) - The lag between cause and visible effect

### Key Concepts
- **Three Observable Anomalies**: Economic growth deceleration, capital concentration, crisis clustering (all quantified and tested)
- **Three Falsifiable Predictions**: Growth cap (S-curve), substrate concentration, crisis lag
- **Crisis Propagation Chain**: How environmental density → adaptation cost → system collapse
- **Horizon of Influence**: Why global crises take 11±2 years to manifest

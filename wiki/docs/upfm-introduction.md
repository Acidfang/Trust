## Self-Consistency Check 1: Do the Three Irreducibles Contradict?

**Irreducible 1 (Is/Is not) + Irreducible 2 (Was/Was not): Do they contradict?**

- Irreducible 1 states: Difference can exist (asymmetry in state space)
- Irreducible 2 states: Difference resolves spontaneously (moves toward sameness)
- **Question**: If difference exists, and then resolves, can new difference emerge? 
- **Answer**: YES. Irreducible 3 (persistence) allows the resolved state to contain new difference

**No contradiction.** They are complementary: 
1. Difference appears
2. Difference resolves
3. Resolved state persists, can contain new difference
4. Cycle repeats

**Irreducible 3 (Persisted/Not persisted) + Irreducibles 1 & 2: Contradiction?**

- Irreducible 3 states: Resolved patterns can persist
- Irreducibles 1 & 2 state: Difference appears and resolves
- **Question**: If patterns persist, do new differences keep appearing?
- **Answer**: YES. Persistence preserves pattern structure, but structure itself can contain new asymmetries
- **Example**: Water molecule persists (pattern repeats), but water has internal electron gradients (new differences within persistence)

**No contradiction.** All three work together:
- (1) Creates asymmetry
- (2) Drives resolution
- (3) Stabilizes resolved state
- Then cycle continues within preserved structure

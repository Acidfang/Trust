# Everything You Shouldn't Do: The Self-Sabotage Edition

**All antipatterns apply to systems AND to yourself. You do these to yourself systematically.**

---

## ANTIPATTERN 1: UNNECESSARY ABSTRACTION (On Yourself)

**What you do:** Talk to therapist instead of feeling your feelings directly.

"I need to process this."

You put a layer between you and experience. By the time you've explained it, you've already interpreted it. You're not feeling. You're narrating.

Therapist isn't the problem. *Using therapist to avoid direct experience* is the problem.

**You're tier 1 boss**
→ Filter to yourself
→ Lose the signal

Same as your code adding unnecessary layer between caller and implementation.

You moved one layer away from reality.

---

## ANTIPATTERN 2: PREMATURE OPTIMIZATION (On Yourself)

**What you do:** Optimize your appearance before you've figured out who you are.

"I need to be attractive first, then I can be myself."

You're speeding up function that uses 1% of the problem.

Who you are matters more than how you look. Optimize for internal first. External follows.

Instead you do the opposite. Dress right. Say the right things. Sound smart. Then wonder why nobody knows you.

You optimized the wrong variable.

**Metabolism equivalent:** You cut your calories to dangerous levels to lose weight fast. Your metabolism breaks. You gain it back. Forever slower metabolism.

Same failure. You optimized for speed. Destroyed the system.

## ANTIPATTERN 3: TIGHT COUPLING (On Yourself)

**What you do:** Make your identity dependent on one person's approval.

"I am what they think I am."

If they leave, you're gone too.

You've coupled your existence to external validation.

Remove the person. The system (you) collapses.
